Salt Scan — Support

Need help with Salt Scan? You're in the right place.

Quick Start

  1. Open the Scan tab and point the camera at a product barcode.
  2. Read the rating: Low, Medium or High, with the sodium or salt per 100 g and per serving.
  3. Tap Add to today's journal and pick servings or grams.
  4. On Home, tap the ring to open your journal. Use the ‹ › arrows to go back to earlier days, swipe a portion to delete it, or tap it to edit it.
  5. In Settings, set your daily goal and your unit (sodium in mg or salt in g).

Frequently Asked Questions

What is SaltScan?

SaltScan is an app designed to help you check the sodium content in food products by scanning the product's barcode. It allows you to make informed choices about the products you consume.

Do I need to create an account to use SaltScan?

No, SaltScan does not require you to create an account. You can use the app without signing in.

Why does SaltScan request access to my camera?

SaltScan requests access to your camera solely to scan food products. We do not collect or store any images or personal data from your camera.

Is my personal data collected by SaltScan?

No, SaltScan does not collect any personal information. The app only uses your camera to scan products, and no data is shared or stored. For exactly what is sent when you look up a product, see the Privacy Policy.

How can I disable camera access in SaltScan?

You can disable camera access at any time by going to your device’s settings and turning off camera access for the SaltScan app.

How accurate is the information provided by SaltScan?

The accuracy of the data depends on the product’s barcode and the information available in our database. We strive to provide accurate information, but we recommend checking labels for confirmation.

Can I scan products that are not in the database?

If a product is not found, you can search it by name from the Home screen, or add it to Open Food Facts, the open database Salt Scan relies on. New products usually appear within a day.

How do I contact customer support?

If you have any questions or need support, you can contact us at trabelsiachraf.devapps@gmail.com.

Is SaltScan available in multiple languages?

Salt Scan is available in English, French and Arabic, and follows your device language.

Can I request to add a product to the database?

Yes, you can suggest a product to be added by contacting our support team at trabelsiachraf.devapps@gmail.com. We review all suggestions regularly.

How do I change or delete a portion?

Tap your daily ring on Home, or a portion in the Today section, to open your journal. Tap a portion to change its amount or its day, or swipe it to the left to delete it. Use the arrows at the top to go to another day, and + to add a portion to that day.

Can Salt Scan save to Apple Health?

Yes. Turn on "Save to Apple Health" in Settings. Salt Scan then saves the sodium of each portion to the Health app, updates it when you edit a portion and removes it when you delete one. It never reads your Health data, and nothing leaves your device.
