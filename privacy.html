Salt Scan — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

What stays on your device

Your journal, your history, your favourites, your daily goal and the reason you gave for watching salt are stored only on your iPhone, in its widgets and on your paired Apple Watch. We have no account system and no server that receives them.

Product lookups

To show a product, Salt Scan sends its barcode, or the words you type in the search, to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org), a public food database. If Open Food Facts cannot answer, the barcode is looked up in our own product list hosted on Google Firebase. These requests carry no name, account or identifier of you. To find less salty alternatives, Salt Scan sends the product's category to Open Food Facts.

Camera

The camera is used to scan barcodes and, if you choose, to read a nutrition label. Label photos are read on your device and are never sent anywhere.

Anonymous statistics

Salt Scan sends anonymous usage statistics to TelemetryDeck (telemetrydeck.com) to learn which features are used, for example that a product was scanned or that a portion was added. They never include what you scan, search or eat, your goal, or why you watch salt. TelemetryDeck stores no IP address and only a hashed device identifier that cannot identify you. Each statistic also carries general technical context: device model, system version, language and region, time zone and time of day, display and accessibility settings (such as text size), and app sessions (when the app is opened and for how long). You can turn the statistics off at any time in Settings › Share anonymous statistics.

Apple Health

If you turn on "Save to Apple Health" in Settings, Salt Scan writes the sodium of each journal portion to the Health app on your device, updates it when you edit a portion and removes it when you delete one. Salt Scan never reads your Health data, and none of it is sent to us or to anyone else. You can turn this off at any time in Settings, or in the Health app.

Reminders

The evening reminder is a notification scheduled on your device, only if you turn it on.

Tracking and advertising

Salt Scan shows no ads and does not track you across apps or websites.

Changes

We will update this page when the app changes what it sends. The date at the top shows the last change.

Contact

Questions about this policy: trabelsiachraf.devapps@gmail.com, or Settings › Contact us.
